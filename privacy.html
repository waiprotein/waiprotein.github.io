Wai Clips Privacy Terms

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how Wai Clips ("the app") handles information when you connect a Google account. Wai Clips is operated by Wai Luc and runs on the operator's own computer.

Google account data the app accesses

When you connect YouTube, the app asks Google for two permissions:

  • youtube.upload: to upload video clips you choose to your YouTube channel and set their title, description and visibility.
  • youtube.readonly: to read your channel's name and ID so you can see which channel is connected, and to read view, like and comment counts for videos the app uploaded.

The app does not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive files or any other Google data, and cannot delete your videos.

How the data is used

  • Upload clips to your channel when you, or a schedule you set up, ask it to.
  • Show which channel is connected and the status of each upload.
  • Show performance statistics for uploaded clips and use them to choose better clips in the future.

The data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Storage and security

OAuth tokens, channel details and clip statistics are stored in a database on the operator's own computer. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest. Nothing is stored on third-party servers by the app itself, apart from the videos you choose to upload to YouTube.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared with anyone, except Google/YouTube itself when uploading a video or requesting statistics.

Transcripts of the videos being clipped are sent to AssemblyAI (speech-to-text) and to Google Gemini (choosing clip moments). These services process video content only. They do not receive your Google account data or OAuth tokens.

Limited Use

Wai Clips' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Wai Clips uses YouTube API Services, and by using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your account stays connected. To remove access at any time:

  • Disconnect YouTube in the app's Social Accounts settings, which deletes the stored tokens; or
  • Revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

To have all stored data about your account deleted, email the address below and it will be removed within 30 days.
