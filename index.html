Wai Clips

Wai Clips is a personal, self-hosted tool that turns long-form videos into short vertical clips with subtitles, and uploads them as YouTube Shorts to its owner's own YouTube channel.

What it does

  • Transcribes a long video and picks the strongest moments.
  • Renders them as 9:16 clips with word-synced captions.
  • With your permission, uploads selected clips to your YouTube channel and reads basic statistics (views, likes, comments) for clips it uploaded.

Who it's for

Wai Clips runs on its owner's own computer and is not offered as a public service. It is built on SupoClip, open-source software licensed under AGPL-3.0, and is not affiliated with SupoClip, YouTube or Google.
